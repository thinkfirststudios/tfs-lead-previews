The buying path
  1. 1

    Pre-approval

    A lender reviews income, assets and credit and indicates what it may lend. It sets a realistic range and shows sellers you are prepared.

  2. 2

    What inventory looks like

    A clear picture of the housing stock in the areas you are considering. [CONFIRM market]

  3. 3

    Touring

    Shortlisted homes are viewed in person, with notes on condition, layout, lot and anything worth a closer look.

  4. 4

    Offer strategy

    Price, terms, contingencies and timing are shaped using recent comparable sales and the specifics of the home.

  5. 5

    Attorney review

    New Jersey residential contracts prepared by licensees generally include an attorney-review period after signing, during which either party's attorney may review and cancel within the stated window. [CONFIRM]

  6. 6

    Inspection

    A licensed inspector reports on the home's visible systems and structure; next steps follow the contract's terms.

  7. 7

    Closing

    Final walk-through, loan funding, title transfer and keys. [CONFIRM local closing customs]
