The lifecycle of a device

Every phone gets a second life.

The four steps behind every UP device, from someone's drawer to its next home.

  1. Trade-in

    We take back your old handsets and accessories, so they're reused or recycled instead of sitting in a drawer.

  2. Data wipe

    We refuse to let your personal data or intellectual property be compromised. Every device is fully erased first.

  3. UPgrade

    Our Phone Doctors test and restore each device to perfect working condition, ready to be used again.

  4. Find the next home

    We find the right channel for every phone, getting the best value for it and giving it a second life.
