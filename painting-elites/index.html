Two different problems

Interior vs. Exterior

Interior

The enemy is dust, odour and disruption — you are living in the job site.

What's involved
Furniture moved and covered, floors papered, patching and spot-priming, cut-in by hand, two finish coats.
Typical timeline
[CONFIRM typical interior timeline]
Prep differences
Nail pops, drywall dings and previous touch-up patches all telegraph through a fresh coat unless they're sanded and primed first.
Season
Year-round. Ventilation matters more than weather.

Exterior

The enemy is sun, moisture and substrate movement — Southern California is hard on a south elevation.

What's involved
Pressure wash, stucco crack repair, caulking at penetrations, masonry primer, elastomeric or acrylic finish.

Prep differences
Chalking, efflorescence and failed caulk have to be dealt with or the new coat fails at the same seams as the old one.
Season
SoCal allows most of the year, but surface temperature and dew point still govern recoat windows.

Advisory, not just labour

Choosing a Sheen

The sheen decides how a room reads and how well it survives being lived in. Most repaint regret is a sheen problem, not a colour problem.

Matte / Flat

Hides wall imperfection best, reflects almost nothing. Ceilings and low-traffic adult bedrooms. Hardest to clean.

Eggshell

The default for living areas. Slight sheen, wipes down, still forgiving on an older wall.

Satin

Kitchens, hallways, bathrooms. Takes scrubbing, but shows any patch that wasn't sanded flat.

Semi-Gloss

Trim, doors, casings and cabinetry. Hard, cleanable, and it deliberately catches light so millwork reads as millwork.

How a job runs

Our Process

  1. 01

    Estimate

    We walk the property, measure, and note substrate condition before quoting anything.

  2. 02

    Written scope & colour sign-off

    Every surface listed, every colour and sheen agreed in writing before a lid comes off.

  3. 03

    Prep & masking

    Wash, patch, caulk, sand, prime. This is the stage that decides how the job looks in year five.

  4. 04

    Application

    Brush, roll or spray to suit the surface, with the recoat window respected between coats.

  5. 05

    Final walkthrough

    We walk it with you in daylight and correct anything you point at before we load the truck.
