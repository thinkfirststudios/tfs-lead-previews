“Clean” means clean

One bag of trash in one load can contaminate an entire batch of compost — and in Virginia, “clean” green waste isn’t a marketing word, it’s a condition the facility operates under. Plastic doesn’t break down; treated wood doesn’t belong in soil.

Not sure about your load? Call before you drive out. (540) 500-1602 — a two-minute call beats a wasted trip with a loaded trailer.
