Who does what on the team?

Gloria Carroll [CONFIRM title]. All four hold Pennsylvania real estate licences, and the team works within Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices The Preferred Realty.

How is a four-person team different from a solo agent?

More than one licensed person knows your file. One can be at a showing while another handles inspection deadlines, and a dedicated Transaction Manager tracks the path to closing. [CONFIRM in Linda's words]

How does new-construction representation work?

The builder's on-site sales staff work for the builder. A buyer's agent works for you: reviewing the builder's contract, options and upgrade pricing, timelines and inspection points. Bill Honeywill is the team's New Construction Specialist. [CONFIRM how builder compensation is handled] This is general information, not legal advice.

How do you price a home?

Against recent comparable sales in the same community and housing type, adjusted for condition, lot and location, then reviewed with you before anything is listed. [CONFIRM Linda's pricing process]

Buyers generally sign a written agreement with their agent before touring, and the agreement sets out what the agent is paid and how. [CONFIRM how Linda presents buyer agreements] This is a description of practice, not legal advice.
