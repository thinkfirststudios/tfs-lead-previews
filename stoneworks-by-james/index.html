The craft

Hand-set stone, not glued-on panels

Veneer panels go up fast and come off the same way. Hand-set stone is laid piece by piece into mortar over a proper substrate, so the wall carries itself. On the Gulf Coast that difference matters more than it does most places — heat cycles, salt air, and storm season find every shortcut.

Pavers

Driveways, walkways, patios

Pattern is a real decision, not decoration — it changes how the surface locks together and how it wears.

  • Herringbone Strongest interlock. The default for driveways.
  • Running bond Clean and simple. Good for walkways.
  • Basketweave Traditional, decorative, best on patios.
