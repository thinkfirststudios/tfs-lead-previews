The signature service

Cabinet Refinishing

A kitchen does not need new cabinets to stop looking like 1998. Refinishing keeps the boxes you already have and puts a sprayed factory-grade finish on them — a fraction of the cost of replacement, and a fraction of the disruption.

  1. 01 Degrease — kitchen cabinets carry years of cooking oil; nothing bonds to that.
  2. 02 Sand and de-gloss so the primer has a mechanical key.
  3. 03 Bonding primer chosen for the substrate: oak, maple, thermofoil, laminate.
  4. 04 Sprayed finish — doors off, racked and sprayed, boxes finished on site.
  5. 05 Cure time before reinstall [CONFIRM turnaround]

Standards

Prep Is The Job

Anyone can put colour on a wall. What separates a coatings contractor from a guy with a roller is everything that happens before the finish coat.

01

Masking

Hard lines come from tape pulled at the right time, not from a steady hand.

02

Caulking

Every gap at trim, casing and corner bead closed before primer.

03

Patching

Nail pops, cracks and dents filled, sanded flush, spot-primed.

04

Primer Selection

Bonding, stain-blocking or masonry primer — chosen for the substrate, not for what's on the truck.

05

Cure Times

Recoat windows respected. Rushing the second coat is how a finish fails.
