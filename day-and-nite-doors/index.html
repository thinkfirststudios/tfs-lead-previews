Broken spring

Don't try to lift it.

(714) 961-1141

Availability: [CONFIRM 24/7 availability]

Do not attempt to open the door or repair the spring yourself. A torsion spring stores an enormous amount of energy under tension — enough to cause serious injury when it lets go, and enough that professionals use dedicated winding bars to work on one. If a spring has snapped, leave the door alone, keep people away from it, and call.

What a broken spring looks and sounds like:

  • A loud bang from the garage, often when nobody was operating the door
  • A visible gap in the coil of the spring above the door
  • The opener strains, lifts a few inches, then stops
  • The door feels impossibly heavy if you try it by hand
  • The door drops fast rather than lowering under control
