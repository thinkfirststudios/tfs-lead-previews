What "complete tear-off" means

Six stages, every time. Not a layer-over.

01

Old material removal

Everything comes off, down to the deck.

02

Decking inspection

Soft or rotten sheathing gets found and replaced.

04

Flashing

05

Installed to the manufacturer's spec so the warranty holds.

Cleanup & haul-away

Debris removed and a magnetic nail sweep before we leave.

Why we publish a number

Most roofers won't tell you a price until they're standing in your driveway.

We publish $6.25/sqft because it lets you work out roughly what you're dealing with before you talk to anybody. It's a starting rate, and it's honest about being one.

  • Pitch — steeper roofs cost more to work safely.
  • Existing layers — more tear-off means more labour and disposal.
  • Decking condition — rotten sheathing has to be replaced.
  • Materials — the system you choose moves the number.

No tier pricing is published here. Those four factors are what we'd walk you through on the measure. [CONFIRM]
