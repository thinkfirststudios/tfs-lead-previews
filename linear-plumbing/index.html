Their words, unpacked

What "code-trained" buys you

It is a phrase most homeowners skim past. Here is what it actually changes about the job.

  • The permit gets pulledWork that legally needs a permit gets one, so the job exists on the record instead of surfacing during your escrow.
  • It's built to be inspectedClearances, venting, supports and materials done to what an inspector is going to look for — not to what is quickest.
  • It passes the first timeA failed inspection is a second visit, a second day off work, and a second hole in the wall.
  • It survives the next ownerUnpermitted plumbing is a line item on somebody's inspection report eventually. Usually yours.
