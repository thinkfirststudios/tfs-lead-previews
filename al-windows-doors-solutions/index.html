The part that matters

Professional installation

A good window fitted badly performs worse than an average window fitted properly. This is the step that separates a real installer from a big-box subcontract.

  1. Measure — the opening, squarely, before anything is ordered.
  2. Removal — old unit out without wrecking the surround.
  3. Flashing & sealing — the bit nobody sees and everything depends on.
  4. Insulation — the gap packed properly, not stuffed.
  5. Trim — inside and out, finished to match.
  6. Cleanup & haul-away — old units taken with us.

Lead-safe work: replacing windows on homes built before 1978 triggers EPA RRP lead-safe certification requirements. [CONFIRM EPA RRP certification] — a genuine legal requirement and a real differentiator once verified.
