Don't replace it — repair it

Most cleaning companies only clean. We also repair seams, patch burns and pet damage, and power-stretch rippled carpet flat.

  • Ripples and waves — usually from humidity, or a carpet that was never stretched properly on install.
  • Split seams — where two pieces meet and the join has come apart.
  • Burns and pet damage — patched with carpet taken from a closet so the repair disappears.
