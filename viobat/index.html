The one nobody explains

Water pressure problems

"Water pressure" is on our list as a service because it is a real, diagnosable problem — not a vague complaint. There are three different versions of it, and they have different causes.

Low pressure

Water comes out, but weakly. Usually a restriction somewhere — a partly closed valve, a blocked aerator or filter, corroded pipe, or a supply issue upstream.

No pressure

Nothing at all, or a trickle. Often a closed or failed valve, an empty tank, a pump that has stopped, or a break in the line.

Pressure that comes and goes

Strong sometimes, weak others. Points at a pump cycling, a tank filling and emptying, a failing pressure valve, or demand elsewhere on the system.

Before it gets expensive

Leak detection

A hidden leak runs day and night. The signs are usually there before the damage is.

  • A bill that went up without anything changing in how you use water.
  • A damp patch on a wall or floor that keeps coming back after it dries.
  • The sound of running water when every tap is closed.
  • Mould or a musty smell with nothing visibly dripping.
  • Ground that stays wet outside when it hasn't rained.

Common and fixable

Toilet repairs

  • Running constantly — usually the flush valve or fill valve inside the cistern.
  • Blocked — and whether it is the pan itself or further down the line.
  • Cistern faults — not filling, filling too slowly, or overflowing.
  • Leaking at the base — often the seal, and worth fixing before the floor is damaged.
  • Replacement — when repairing costs more than it's worth.
