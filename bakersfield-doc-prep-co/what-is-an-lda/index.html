What is a Legal Document Assistant?

The short answer: someone registered and bonded under California law to prepare legal documents for people who are representing themselves.

The role, in one paragraph

California law recognises that a lot of people go to court without an attorney. Sometimes because they cannot afford one, sometimes because the matter is straightforward and they have already decided what they want to do. Those people still have to file documents, and the documents still have to be right.

A Legal Document Assistant is registered with the county clerk and bonded, and is authorised to prepare those documents — at the direction of the person filing them. You decide. We prepare.

What that means in practice

We can

  • Prepare the documents you tell us you want prepared
  • Complete them correctly and in the right order
  • Tell you what our fee is, up front
  • Provide you with a written agreement
  • Give you general information about how a process works

We cannot

  • Give you legal advice
  • Tell you which documents you should file
  • Tell you what to ask the court for
  • Represent you, or speak for you in court
  • Tell you whether you are likely to succeed

The line between those two lists

It comes down to one distinction: information about how a process generally works is not advice. Telling you what to do in your situation is.

So “here is what the form asks for” is something we can say. “You should ask for this instead” is not — not because we are being careful, but because it would be practising law without a licence.

How an LDA differs from an attorney

  • An attorney advises you on what to do, represents you, and speaks for you in court. They carry professional responsibility for the advice they give, and your conversations with them are privileged.
  • An LDA prepares documents at your direction. No advice, no representation, and — importantly — no privilege. What you tell us is not protected the way it would be with an attorney.

That last point is worth sitting with, because people are often surprised by it. It is one reason we ask you not to send case details through the contact form.

What registration and bonding actually mean

When you should hire an attorney instead

This is the part most sites in this category leave out, so here it is properly.

  • You need advice, not documents. If the question in your head is “what should I do?” rather than “can you prepare this?”, an LDA cannot help you and an attorney can.
  • The matter is contested. If the other side disagrees and it is heading for a fight, you want someone who can advocate for you.
  • Custody is in dispute. Whatever else is true, this one deserves an attorney.
  • There is domestic violence involved. Please speak to an attorney and to the court’s self-help resources.
  • Property division is complicated. Businesses, pensions, multiple properties, anything with real disagreement attached.
  • It is a criminal matter beyond record relief. An active case is an attorney’s job.
