The most important thing on this site

What is a Legal Document Assistant?

What an LDA is

A Legal Document Assistant is a registered and bonded professional, authorised under California law, to prepare legal documents for people who are representing themselves.

The key words are at your direction. You decide what you are filing. We prepare it properly.

What an LDA is not

An LDA is not an attorney. That means we cannot:

  • Give you legal advice
  • Tell you what to file, or what to ask for
  • Represent you, or appear in court on your behalf
  • Tell you how to argue your case

An honest section

Should you hire an attorney instead?

Sometimes, yes. Here is when — and we would rather tell you now than take your money first.

  • Contested custody. If you and the other parent do not agree, that is not a paperwork problem.
  • Complex property division. Businesses, pensions, multiple properties, anything with real disagreement attached.
  • Anything involving domestic violence. Please speak to an attorney, and to the court’s self-help resources.
  • Criminal matters beyond record relief. An active case is an attorney’s job.
  • Any situation where you need advice about what to do rather than help preparing what you have already decided to do. That distinction is the whole line between what an attorney does and what we do.
