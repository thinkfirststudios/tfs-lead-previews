The problem

Water Has to Go Somewhere

  • Foundation erosion — water pooling at the base of the house.
  • Stained stucco — dark streaks running down the wall below the roofline.
  • Rotted fascia — the wood behind the gutter line going soft.
  • Hillside runoff — a real issue on SoCal slopes.
  • Post-fire debris flow — burned slopes shed water and mud fast.

Rain gutters

Seamless vs. sectional

Sectional gutters are joined every few feet, and every joint is a future leak. A seamless gutter is formed in one continuous piece to the length of your roofline — the only joins are at corners and outlets.

Chimney caps

What a cap actually does

  • Rain — keeps water out of the flue.
  • Animals — keeps birds and other animals out.
  • Sparks — the spark arrestor mesh contains embers.
  • Downdraft — reduces smoke pushed back into the room.

Copping / coping

The word nobody knows

A metal coping cap is the capping that covers the top of a parapet wall — the low wall around a flat roof. Without it, water sits on the exposed masonry and works its way in.
