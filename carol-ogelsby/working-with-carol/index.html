The process

Five steps, in order.

  1. Step 01

    First conversation

    What you hope to do, when, and what you want to know before deciding anything.

  2. Step 02

    Preparation & pricing strategy

    Recent comparable sales, the condition of the house, and a plan for what to fix and what to leave.

  3. Step 03

    Marketing & access

    Photography, presentation and showings for sellers; tours and disclosures for buyers.

  4. Step 04

    Offer & negotiation

    Terms, contingencies and inspection responses, negotiated in writing.

  5. Step 05

    The settlement table
