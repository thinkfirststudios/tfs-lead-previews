Radnor Township

Stone and stucco houses on mature lots, the Lancaster Avenue business district in Wayne, and a string of Regional Rail stations. [CONFIRM copy]

The place

Houses and streetscape

Much of the township's housing stock is early twentieth-century: stone and stucco Colonial Revival houses, Tudor-influenced houses with slate roofs, and twins and smaller houses closer to the rail line, set among mature trees on lots that widen further from Lancaster Avenue. Villages within the township

Wayne, Villanova and St. Davids.

Wayne

A walkable Lancaster Avenue centre around the station, with streets of older stone, stucco and frame houses radiating out from it. [CONFIRM copy]

Villanova

Larger lots and winding roads, stone walls and long driveways; the village also extends into Lower Merion Township. [CONFIRM copy]

St. Davids

Wooded, hilly streets between Wayne and Radnor, with its own station on the Paoli/Thorndale Line. [CONFIRM copy]
