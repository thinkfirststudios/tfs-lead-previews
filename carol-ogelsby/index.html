What does the first conversation look like?

A conversation rather than a presentation: what you are hoping to do, on what timeline, and what you would like to understand before deciding anything. [CONFIRM how Carol runs a first meeting — office, phone, or at the property]

How is pricing set on the Main Line?

From recent comparable sales, the condition and setting of the house, and what is currently competing for the same buyers — shown to you as evidence rather than a single number. [CONFIRM Carol's pricing approach in her own words]

What does a buyer's agent do, and how is Carol paid?

A buyer's agent represents your interests in finding, evaluating and negotiating for a home. The terms of that representation, including compensation, are set out in a written agreement. [CONFIRM exactly what Carol will state publicly about buyer agreements and compensation]

How should I research schools?

Carol does not offer opinions on schools.

School assignments and boundaries change. Buyers should verify district information and any school-related questions independently with the district and the Pennsylvania Department of Education.

Each public school district publishes its own boundary and enrolment information on its own website; the Pennsylvania Department of Education maintains the statewide directory of districts. [CONFIRM links]
