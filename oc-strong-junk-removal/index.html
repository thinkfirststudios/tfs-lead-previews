    What to photograph

    Four things that make your quote accurate.

    None of this is fussy. It just means the price we send back is the price that holds up when we arrive.

    Get the whole pile in frame

    One wide shot beats five close-ups. If it doesn’t fit, step back rather than cropping the edges off.

    Include something for scale

    A door, a car, a person standing beside it. Piles are almost impossible to size from a photo without one.

    Shoot from a few feet back

    Close-ups hide the depth of a pile. Distance shows us how much is actually behind the front row.

    Show the path out

    Stairs, a side gate, a long driveway. The route matters as much as the pile — it’s where the labour goes.
