What is a Broker Associate, and why does it matter?

In New Jersey a broker associate holds a broker’s licence but works under another broker’s brokerage. It generally involves more pre-licensing education and a broker’s examination than a salesperson licence. How does attorney review work in a New Jersey sale?

Many New Jersey residential contracts include a three-business-day attorney review period, during which either party’s attorney can review the contract before it becomes binding.
