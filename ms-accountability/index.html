What the monthly fee actually buys

The most common reason people cancel a bookkeeping service is that they never worked out what it was doing. So here is the month.

  • Through the month
    Transactions categorised

    Every transaction that hits the accounts gets coded to the right place, as it happens rather than in a panic in January.

  • After month end
    Accounts reconciled

    Your books get matched against the bank and card statements, so the numbers in the software are the numbers that actually happened.

The credential line — answer this first

[CONFIRM whether Sharon is a CPA, an Enrolled Agent, or a non-credentialed bookkeeper]

In Washington, “CPA” is a legally protected title under the Public Accountancy Act — only a current licensee may use it or hold out as one. “Enrolled Agent” is a federal IRS designation with the same constraint. Being a non-credentialed bookkeeper is entirely legitimate and extremely common; it simply means the site says “bookkeeper” and describes bookkeeping confidently on its own terms.

This whole build is written to that most conservative case. If Sharon holds a credential, the copy gains latitude rather than losing it — which is the safe direction to edit in.
