Before we get there

What to do right now

Pick what's happening. These steps genuinely change the outcome — and several of them protect your insurance claim.

Water damage — do this now

  1. 1Shut the water off at the main. If you don't know where it is, look for the valve where the supply enters the house or at the meter.
  2. 2Kill power to the affected areas only if the breaker is safe to reach. If you would have to stand in water to get there, don't — wait for us or the utility.
  3. 3Do not use a household vacuum on standing water. It is not rated for it and it is an electrocution risk.
  4. 4Lift furniture legs onto foil or blocks so the stain doesn't wick up into the frame.
  5. 5Pull rugs off wet carpet — the dyes transfer and the damage becomes permanent.
  6. 6Photograph everything before you move it. This is the record your claim will be built on.

Then call 855-VET-7737.

Water damage

Water damage restoration

Speed is the whole job. Mold growth begins within 24 to 48 hours of a material staying wet — that is an industry-standard timeframe, and it is why the first call matters more than the fifth.

  • Common causes — burst pipe, slab leak, water heater failure, an appliance supply line, or storm intrusion.
  • Extraction — standing water removed before anything else happens.
  • Structural drying — air movers and dehumidifiers set to a drying plan, not just pointed at the wet bit.
  • Moisture mapping — readings logged daily so there is evidence the structure actually dried.

Fire & smoke

Fire damage restoration

Most of what a fire ruins, it ruins after the flames are out. Soot is acidic and keeps etching surfaces; smoke odour keeps migrating through the structure.

  • Structural cleanup — removing what cannot be saved so what can be saved stops degrading.
  • Soot and smoke odour removal — surfaces, cavities and the parts of the building you can't see.

Mold

Mold remediation

Mold is a moisture problem wearing a mold costume. Removing growth without finding and stopping the water source guarantees it comes back.

  • Containment — sheeting and a zipper door so spores don't travel while the work happens.
  • Negative air and HEPA filtration — the room is held at lower pressure than the rest of the house.
  • Source correction — the leak gets fixed, or the job is not finished.
