Maintenance

The visit that prevents the emergency

A garage door is the largest moving object in most houses, and it runs on parts that wear out on a schedule.

  • Tune-up and hardware tightening
  • Lubrication of rollers, hinges and springs
  • Balance check — the door should hold position halfway when disconnected
  • Safety sensor alignment and reverse test
  • Cable and spring inspection before they fail rather than after
