What a title search and title insurance actually do

Guide 01 · How a Sale Actually Works in Ohio

General information, not legal advice. Every Ohio-specific procedure, cost and custom below is [CONFIRM] before publication.

The short version

Before a house changes hands, someone checks that the seller actually has the right to sell it and that nothing recorded against the property — a mortgage, a lien, an easement — will follow it to the new owner. That check is the title search. Title insurance protects against problems the search missed.

What a title search looks at

Recorded documents tied to the property: past deeds, mortgages and their releases, liens, judgments, easements and restrictions. [CONFIRM which county offices and records apply in Summit and Stark counties]
