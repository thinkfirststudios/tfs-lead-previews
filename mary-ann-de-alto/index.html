How it works

The Easiest Method To Find a House

Three plain processes. Each step is what happens — not a promise about how long it takes or how it turns out.

  1. Pre-approval

    Talk to a lender about what you can borrow before touring. A pre-approval letter shows a seller you're ready to make an offer.

  2. Search

    Set your must-haves and the towns you're considering, then tour the homes that fit.

  3. Offer

    A written offer sets price, terms and contingencies. Your agent presents it and negotiates with the seller's side.

  4. Inspection

    A licensed inspector reviews the home's condition. Findings can lead to repair requests, credits or renegotiation.

  5. Closing

    A final walk-through, then documents are signed and funds transferred. Ownership passes and the keys change hands.

  1. Prep

    Walk the home together, decide what's worth fixing or staging, and schedule photography.

  2. Pricing

    A comparative look at recent nearby sales and current competition sets a list price you understand.

  3. Marketing

    The listing goes live with photos and details, followed by showings and open houses. [CONFIRM MLS]

  4. Negotiation

    Offers are reviewed side by side — price, terms, contingencies — and negotiated toward one you accept.

  5. Closing

    Inspection and appraisal items are worked through, documents signed, and the sale completes.

  1. Search

    Set a monthly budget, move-in date and location, then tour available rentals.

  2. Application

    Submit the landlord's application. Screening follows the landlord's written criteria, applied the same way to every applicant.

  3. Lease

    Review the lease terms — length, rent, deposit, responsibilities — before signing.

  4. Move-in

    Walk through and document the unit's condition, collect keys, and move in.
