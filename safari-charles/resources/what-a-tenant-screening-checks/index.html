What a tenant screening actually checks

For owners and applicants alike. General information about how screening works — not legal advice.

General information about how things work in this market, not legal advice. Specific rules, limits and procedures are marked [CONFIRM] until verified. Consult a Maryland attorney about your situation.

Why screening criteria are written down

A screening is fair only if every applicant for a home is measured against the same criteria, applied in the same order. Written criteria, shared with applicants before they apply, protect both the applicant and the owner.

What is typically reviewed

  • Identity verification — confirming the applicant is who the application says.
  • Income and ability to pay — reviewed consistently; lawful sources of income, including housing assistance, are counted where the law requires. [CONFIRM county source-of-income rules]
  • Rental history — prior tenancies and landlord references.
  • Credit report — with the applicant's written consent. [CONFIRM what is checked]
  • Other background checks — only where lawful and applied uniformly. [CONFIRM Maryland and county restrictions]
