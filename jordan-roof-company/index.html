Two roofs, two arguments

Shingle vs. Tile

Drag the divider, or tap either side. Comparative figures are deliberately left out until we can state them accurately for your roof. [CONFIRM]

Shingle

  • Weight Light — most framing takes it without modification.
  • Cost profile Lower up front.
  • Repair Individual shingles swap out easily.
  • Orange County Handles sun and wind well with the right underlayment.

Tile

  • Weight Heavy — framing has to be rated for it.
  • Cost profile Higher up front, longer service life.
  • Repair Tile is often salvageable and can be re-laid.
  • Orange County The regional default, and it suits the architecture.

Santa Ana season

Wind & storm damage

Santa Ana winds don't usually take a whole roof off. They lift edges, crack tile, back out fasteners and open up flashing — damage that does nothing until the first real rain, then does a lot all at once.

If you're filing with a carrier, what they generally want is documentation: dated photographs, the location of the damage, and a written scope. That's exactly what the free inspection produces.
