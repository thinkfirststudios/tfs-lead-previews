When it can't wait

Emergency repairs

Not every plumbing problem is an emergency. These are.

  • Water coming in and you can't stop it
  • No water at all in the house
  • Sewage backing up into a fixture
  • A water heater leaking from the tank itself
  • Anything wet near an electrical panel or outlet
Before you call: find the shut-off. Most homes have a main shut-off where the supply enters, and most fixtures have their own valve underneath. Turning off the water first limits the damage while you wait — and it is worth knowing where yours is before you need it.

Highest-ticket job on the list

Water heaters

A water heater gives you warning before it fails. Most people miss it.

  • Repair or replace? Rusty hot water, a rumbling tank, or water pooling under it usually means replace. A failed element or thermostat usually means repair.
  • Tank vs tankless. Tankless takes less space and heats on demand; tank is cheaper to install and simpler to service. [CONFIRM whether they install tankless]
  • Sizing. Too small and you run out mid-shower; too large and you pay to keep water hot nobody uses.

The one that keeps coming back

Drain cleaning

If the same drain clogs twice, the clog is not the problem.

  • Slow drain in one fixture — usually local, usually simple.
  • Gurgling — air being pulled through the trap. Often a venting issue, not a blockage.
  • Multiple fixtures backing up at once — that is the main line, and it needs looking at properly rather than plunging.
  • Recurring clogs in the same place — roots, a belly in the line, or a section that has partially collapsed.

One capability of six

Leak detection

Part of a full-service shop rather than the whole identity — but the signs are worth knowing.

  • A bill that climbed with no change in how you use water.
  • A damp patch on a wall, ceiling or baseboard that keeps returning after it dries.
  • Running water you can hear with every tap closed.
  • A warm spot on the floor — often a hot line under the slab.
  • Mould or a musty smell with no visible source.
