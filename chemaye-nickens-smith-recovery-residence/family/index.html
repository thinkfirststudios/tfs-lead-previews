What a recovery residence is.

It is a shared, substance-free house for adults in recovery. Residents live under written house rules, share chores, and pay a fee.

It is not treatment. There is no detox, clinical care, therapy or medical supervision at the house. Those are provided elsewhere, by licensed services. The SAMHSA Helpline above can help you find them.

Read more about what this is and isn’t

What you will and won’t be told.

Residents are adults, and their privacy is protected. Without their written permission, we can’t confirm whether someone lives here or tell you how they are doing.

We know that is hard to hear when you are worried. It is the same protection you would want for yourself. If your family member agrees, they can give written permission for us to speak with you. [CONFIRM her consent-to-share process]
