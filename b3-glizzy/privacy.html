Who we are

Second Shift Songs LLC runs this site, the email list and the text list for B3 GLIZZY. Postal address: 2600 Navigation Blvd, Suite 000, Houston, TX 77003 (placeholder address). It is in the footer of every email.

Email list

You join only by ticking the email box, which is never ticked for you. Double opt-in: one confirmation email, nothing else until you click it. We keep the time, IP address, exact wording shown and which form you used. Emails are sent by our email service provider, [ESP NAME — confirmed at launch], as our processor. Unsubscribe from any email in one click; we honour it within 10 days, usually at once. Unsubscribed addresses stay only on a suppression list. Inactive subscribers are removed after 24 months. EU and UK subscribers: our lawful basis is consent, and you can ask for access or deletion at any time.

Text list

Texts are separate from email, with their own box and their own consent. They are sent by our SMS provider, [SMS PROVIDER — confirmed at launch], as our processor. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, START to rejoin, HELP for help. We keep the consent record (time, IP, exact wording shown). Your number is never sold or shared for anyone else's marketing. Full terms: SMS terms.

Store

Purchases happen on the hosted store platform, which has its own privacy policy. This site never sees card details or addresses. Buying something never adds you to the email or text list.

Embedded players

YouTube, Spotify, Apple Music and Bandcamp players load only when you press play, so they set no cookies before that. YouTube uses privacy-enhanced mode.

Ticket links

Ticket links go to each venue's official ticketing partner. We receive no purchase data.

Access and deletion

Write to privacy@b3glizzy.demo. We reply within 30 days.
