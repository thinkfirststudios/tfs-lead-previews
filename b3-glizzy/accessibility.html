B3 GLIZZY / Accessibility

ACCESSIBILITY.

Target: WCAG 2.2 AA.

  • With reduced motion on, the marquee stops, the hero does not wipe or compress, the cover does not tilt, and nothing masks in: every section is shown in its final state.
  • Nothing flashes more than three times a second.
  • No audio or video plays on its own. Every player loads only when you press play and uses the platform's own controls. Videos have real captions on the platform and a full transcript with lyrics on this site.
  • Lyrics and transcripts are real text, never pictures of text.
  • The orange accent is never used as text on the light background, because it would not be readable enough. It only appears behind dark or light text, or as a line.
  • Ticket and stock states are written in words, not shown by colour alone. Sold-out sizes are struck through and announced as sold out.
  • The stage plots in the press kit have written descriptions and input tables.
  • Keyboard focus is always visible. There is a skip link on every page.

Something not working: management@b3glizzy.demo.
