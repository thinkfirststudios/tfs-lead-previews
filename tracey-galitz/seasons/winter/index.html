What a February showing is actually like

Short daylight, boots at the door and a house seen with the heat on. Snow hides the yard and the roof, but it shows how warm a house stays, whether ice builds at the eaves, and how the driveway and walks handle a Lake County winter.

What a seller should be doing

  • Keep walks, steps and the driveway clear before every showing
  • Watch for ice dams and address attic ventilation
  • Have furnace service records ready
  • Photograph the yard and garden in other seasons to show buyers
  • Note salt damage on concrete and plan spring repairs
