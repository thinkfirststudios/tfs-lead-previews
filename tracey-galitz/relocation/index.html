What a relocation specialist does

A relocation isn't a move. It's a deadline.

The job is sequencing: which decisions come first, which can be made remotely, and which genuinely need you here. The five differences below are why a relocation needs its own plan.

  1. 01The calendar is set by someone else. A start date or a lease ending fixes the timeline before the search even begins.
  2. 02More than one job may be moving. A partner's job search can change where a home needs to be and how far a commute can stretch.
  3. 03There is a gap to bridge. Temporary housing, storage and lease-gap options often run in parallel with the purchase.
  4. 04There is often a home to sell somewhere else. In another state, on another market's timetable, while you're already here.
  5. 05Decisions get made from 800 miles away. Video walkthroughs, remote inspections and closings that don't require being in the room.

What is LeadingRE?

Leading Real Estate Companies of the World® is a global network of independent brokerages. When someone leaves one market for another, a member brokerage at the origin can refer them to a member at the destination — so the move is handed over, not started from scratch. Relocation FAQ

Questions people ask before the first call

Review[CONFIRM every answer with Tracey before publishing]

How is a relocation different from an ordinary move?

A relocation is driven by a fixed date — usually a job start — and often involves temporary housing, a home to sell in another state, and decisions made from a distance. The plan works backwards from that date.

Can I buy in Lake County without flying in for every step?

Much of the process can be handled remotely — video walkthroughs, digital documents and remote-closing options. What is possible depends on the title company and lender involved. [CONFIRM what Tracey and the title companies she uses support]

My employer uses a relocation management company. Can you work with them?

Relocation work often arrives through relocation management companies and in-house mobility teams, and the HR door on this site is built for exactly that. [CONFIRM how Tracey works with RMCs and which relationships she has]

What is LeadingRE?

Leading Real Estate Companies of the World® is a global network of independent brokerages that refer clients to one another when they move between markets. [CONFIRM wording + LeadingRE permission]

I'm moving between Illinois and Wisconsin. Do I need two agents?

Not necessarily. An agent licensed in both states may be able to help on both sides, subject to each state's rules and brokerage sponsorship. [CONFIRM WI licence # and Wisconsin sponsoring brokerage]

How do I compare commutes before I visit?

Start with the Metra lines — Milwaukee District North runs out of Libertyville, and the Union Pacific North line serves the lakefront — then check I-94 and Tri-State drive times at the hours you would actually travel. [CONFIRM current schedules + date-stamp]
