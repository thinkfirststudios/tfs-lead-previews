The Relocation Hub

A relocation isn't a move. It's a deadline.

An ordinary move starts with a house. A relocation starts with a date — the day someone is due at a desk in Lake County — and works backwards. A relocation specialist turns that date into a sequence of decisions made in the right order, most of them from somewhere else.

  1. 01The calendar is set by someone else. A start date or a lease ending fixes the timeline before the search even begins.
  2. 02More than one job may be moving. A partner's job search can change where a home needs to be and how far a commute can stretch.
  3. 03There is a gap to bridge. Temporary housing, storage and lease-gap options often run in parallel with the purchase.
  4. 04There is often a home to sell somewhere else. In another state, on another market's timetable, while you're already here.
  5. 05Decisions get made from 800 miles away. Video walkthroughs, remote inspections and closings that don't require being in the room.
