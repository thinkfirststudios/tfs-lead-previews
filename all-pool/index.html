The service

What a closing includes

Four steps are stated in All Pool's own advertising. Each one exists because of something specific that goes wrong without it.

  1. 01

    Blow-out the lines

    Every line between the pool and the equipment pad is cleared of water with compressed air, then plugged so it can't refill.

    Skip it and: water left sitting in a line freezes, expands, and splits the pipe — usually underground, where finding it is most of the cost.

  2. 02

    Balance and add winter chemicals

    Water chemistry is balanced for a long dormant period, and winter chemicals go in.

    Skip it and: unbalanced water sits against the surface for months. You open in spring to staining, scale, or a pool that has gone green before you've touched it.

  3. 03

    Drain and store pump & filter

    Pump, filter and heater are drained down, drain plugs pulled, and equipment stored or left safely dry.

    Skip it and: a pump housing with water still in it cracks when that water freezes. It is the single most common winter casualty on an equipment pad.

  4. 04

    Install the cover

    The cover goes on and gets secured properly for the season.

    Skip it and: leaves, debris and rain go straight in all winter, and you spend the first warm weekend of the year fixing it instead of swimming.

  5. 05

    Final walkthrough

    A last check over the pad and the cover before we leave. [CONFIRM this step exists]

Why early matters

What freezing actually breaks

Described in terms of what fails, not in numbers — no repair costs or statistics appear here, because we don't have real ones.

Cracked lines

Water expands as it freezes. A line with water still in it splits, and the split is usually somewhere you have to dig to reach.

Split pump housings

The pump holds water even when the pool is down. Freeze it and the housing cracks — the whole unit, not a part.

Surface staining

Unbalanced water sitting all winter etches and stains the surface. That damage is cosmetic, permanent, and visible from the first day you open.

Cover options

Three different things get called "a pool cover" and they are not interchangeable. A winter cover is not a safety cover, and this page never describes one as the other.

Safety cover

Mesh, anchored into the deck, held under tension. Designed and rated to hold weight.

Winter cover

Solid sheet held down by water bags around the perimeter. Keeps debris out. Not a safety cover.
