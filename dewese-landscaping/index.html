How often should we come out?

This is the only real decision to make. Pick one and everything below — including the quote form — adjusts to match.

The honest version: in a Maryland spring, grass can outrun a two-week gap — a bi-weekly cut in May takes longer and leaves more clippings than a weekly one. By August it usually doesn’t. Weekly costs more per month and less per visit; one-time costs the most per visit and nothing after that. Nobody needs to be talked into the expensive option.
