Pre-approval

A lender's pre-approval before touring, so the search is set against a real number.

What people get wrong hereTreating a pre-qualification as a pre-approval. They are not the same document.

Search

The 23-town Fairfield County search, narrowed to the towns and property types that actually fit.

What people get wrong hereSearching every town at once instead of deciding on a short list first.

Offer strategy

Price, financing, contingencies and timing weighed together — the offer is more than the number.

What people get wrong hereAssuming the highest price is always the strongest offer.

Inspection & contingencies

Inspections scheduled promptly and contingencies tracked against their deadlines.

What people get wrong hereLetting a contingency deadline pass without a decision.

Closing

Final walk-through, documents and funds, through to the keys.

What people get wrong hereLeaving the final walk-through until there is no time to fix anything.
