Beat 01 · The inn

Days at the inn

Kim is the daughter of Vermont innkeepers. Growing up, she helped her father run the inn — the early starts, the late close, and everything a guest needs in between.

Days started at 6:30am and finished up with shut down around 11pm… not unlike real estate!!— Kim Burke
PLACEHOLDER-story-table.jpg
Beat 02 · The motto

One rule, learned early

Her parents ran the inn on a single rule. In a home sale, the customer is the person at the centre of every call, every showing, every page of paperwork and every decision on the way to closing.

The customer always comes first — it was ingrained in me from an early age.— Kim Burke
PLACEHOLDER-story-pottery.jpg
Beat 03 · Advertising & pottery

A product she marketed herself

After graduating from the College of William and Mary, Kim worked in advertising. Then she opened a pottery business with a gallery in Vermont, running two studios — one in Vermont and one in NYC.

This helped me to gain valuable experience in how to market a product.— Kim Burke

A listing presentation is product marketing. She has done that for a living twice.

PLACEHOLDER-story-door.jpg
Beat 04 · Second generation

A realtor's daughter, too

Kim's mother became a realtor and has owned her own brokerage for many years. Real estate was a family business before it was Kim's.

When Kim chose a brokerage, she chose Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices for its integrity, its marketing materials and its in depth industry statistics.

Beat 05 · Wilton

Fifteen years in the town she sells

15+years in Wilton

Kim moved from NYC to Wilton over fifteen years ago. She raised five children there, including triplets born in 2005 — and it was the way the community showed up for her family after the triplets arrived that made Wilton home for good.

You will know that I have your back every step of the way.— Kim Burke
