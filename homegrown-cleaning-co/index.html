Before you book

What's included in a clean

The single most-searched thing homeowners want to know before booking. [CONFIRM checklist with Shawna] — the lists below are standard practice for the trade, held as a starting point for that conversation.

Kitchen

  • Counters and backsplash wiped down
  • Sink scrubbed and taps polished
  • Cabinet fronts and handles
  • Appliance exteriors, including the fridge front
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped

Bathrooms

  • Shower, tub and tile scrubbed
  • Toilet cleaned inside and out, base included
  • Mirrors and glass left streak-free
  • Vanity, sink and fixtures
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped

Bedrooms

  • Beds made, linens changed if left out
  • Surfaces dusted, including sills and frames
  • Mirrors and glass
  • Floors vacuumed, under the bed where reachable

Living areas

  • Surfaces and shelves dusted
  • Cushions straightened, throws folded
  • Light switches, door handles and remotes wiped
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
