Estate cleanouts

Clearing a parent's house is not a junk job

Most of the time, the person calling us about an estate cleanout has just lost someone. Often they are managing it from another state, on a probate or settlement deadline, in a house they grew up in.

We work at whatever pace that takes.

  • Setting things aside first. Before anything is loaded, you tell us what stays. Photographs, papers, the things that only matter to your family — those get put safely to one side, not sorted at speed.
  • Working with executors, realtors and estate attorneys. If a settlement date is driving the schedule, we work to it and keep whoever needs updating in the loop. [CONFIRM]
  • Donating what's usable. A lot of what's in the house still has life in it, and most families would rather it went somewhere than to a landfill. [CONFIRM donation partners]
  • Whole house, or one room at a time. Some families want it done in a day. Some need to go room by room over weeks. Both are fine.
  • Discretion. No signage on the truck if you'd rather not have it, and nothing discussed with neighbours. [CONFIRM]
