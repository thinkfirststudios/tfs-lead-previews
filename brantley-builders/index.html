Sunrooms

Three-season or four-season

The difference is whether the room is conditioned. A three-season room is glass or screen and gets used most of the year. A four-season room is insulated and tied into heating and cooling, so it counts as living space — and is permitted differently.

[CONFIRM] Which types Brantley builds, glazing and screen options, foundation approach, and how permitting is handled per county.

Decks

Pressure-treated or composite

Pressure-treated costs less up front and needs sealing. Composite costs more and doesn't. Beyond the boards, what actually determines how a deck ages is the framing, the flashing where it meets the house, and the footings.

[CONFIRM materials offered] Railing systems, stairs, multi-level layouts and deck lighting.

Additions

Framing through finish

An addition touches everything — footings, framing, roof tie-in, envelope, mechanical, and finish. The argument for a single contractor is simple: there's nobody to point at when the roofline doesn't meet the existing house properly.
